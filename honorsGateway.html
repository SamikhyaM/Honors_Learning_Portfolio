My First College-Level Hackathon

HackNite — Campus Quest

✳ ───────── ✳

This semester I participated in my first college level hackathon: HackNite. During the 12 hours, I worked on Campus Quest, a project designed to help incoming University of Cincinnati students navigate their first year. My team and I created a website containing resources, tips, and timelines, alongside a Ren'Py game that simulated a student's first day on campus. Players had to manage their energy and money while briefly interacting with other students. I took primary responsibility for conceptualizing and developing the game.

I initially left the experience feeling somewhat disappointed. After an all-nighter of development, our presentation didn't seem to resonate with the judges as much as I had hoped. Looking back, I realized that we hadn't considered our audience enough. As freshmen ourselves, we had designed something for incoming students, but struggled to communicate its relevance to our audience, who consisted of industry professionals and mainly third or fourth year students. However, seeing other projects changed my perspective. The technical/overall winner used virtual motion sensors to animate an electronic puppet in Blender, while the People's Choice winner created a multiplayer virtual ghost gallery. Both of the teams demonstrated how creativity could turn an idea into an engaging experience. It made me appreciate the value of creating something lighthearted and imaginative, where the experience itself is what brings everyone together.

In future projects, I want to be more intentional about understanding my audience, collaborating effectively, and exploring creative approaches to problem-solving. This experience also broadened my understanding of what it means to be a Global Citizen Scholar. I realized that contributing to a community isn’t always through serious problem-solving, like our project. It can also mean recognizing the different experiences people have and creating something that helps them connect, feel welcomed, or just enjoy a shared experience.
